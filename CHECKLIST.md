# New Supplier Onboarding Checklist

Every new supplier must provide the following before a purchase order can be raised.

## Company documents

- [ ] Certificate of incorporation
- [ ] Company registration extract (issued within the last 12 months)
- [ ] Physical and postal address

## Financial documents

- [ ] Bank details on company letterhead, signed and stamped
- [ ] Audited financial statements for the last financial year

## Compliance

- [ ] Signed supplier code of conduct
- [ ] Conflict of interest declaration
