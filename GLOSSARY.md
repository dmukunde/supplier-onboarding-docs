# Incoterms Glossary

Quick reference for the Incoterms 2020 rules most often used in Northwind purchase orders. Incoterms define who pays for transport and insurance, and where risk passes from seller to buyer.

| Term | Full name | Risk passes to buyer | Seller arranges |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EXW | Ex Works | When goods are made available at the seller's premises | Nothing beyond making goods available |
| FCA | Free Carrier | When goods are handed to the buyer's carrier | Export clearance and delivery to the carrier |
| FOB | Free On Board | When goods are loaded on board the vessel | Export clearance and loading at the named port |
| CIF | Cost, Insurance and Freight | When goods are loaded on board the vessel | Freight and minimum insurance to the destination port |
| DAP | Delivered at Place | When goods arrive at the named destination, ready to unload | All transport to the named destination |
| DDP | Delivered Duty Paid | When goods arrive at the named destination, ready to unload | All transport, import clearance and duties |

When raising a purchase order, always state the Incoterm **and** the named place, for example `FOB Mombasa` or `DAP Kampala warehouse`.
